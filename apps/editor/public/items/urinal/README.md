# Wall-mounted urinal

Kohei Ichikawa authored this generic ceramic fixture for Pascal Editor. The mesh
and both images are distributed under the repository's MIT license and were
generated without external meshes or textures. [Upstream commit and author attribution](https://github.com/pascalorg/editor/commit/fa04125495719b4cbd42b3ee0ba9a1a3777e3ba6) · [MIT license](LICENSE)

The model measures 0.40 m wide × 0.65 m high × 0.35 m deep. Its origin is at the
bottom center of the back face and the front points along +Z, matching the
editor's `wall-side` attachment. `slot_ceramic` and `slot_drain` are separate
paintable materials. The transparent floor-plan image shows the back at the top
and the front at the bottom.

The original generator uses Blender 4.5 LTS. [Generator source](https://github.com/pascalorg/editor/blob/fa04125495719b4cbd42b3ee0ba9a1a3777e3ba6/scripts/generate-urinal.py)
